HOW TO PLAY

  • Create a room and share the Room ID, or join a friend's room (2–8 players).
  • Steer with ↑←↓→ or WASD. On a phone, use the on-screen pad or swipe.
  • Eat the glowing food to grow and score 10 points each.
  • Crash into a wall, yourself or another snake and you are out. When the timer ends, the player with the highest score wins.
